The rapid expansion of artificial intelligence has transformed major sectors of the economy since 2020. According to market research, global AI spending reached approximately $100 billion in 2023, representing a year-over-year growth of 38 percent. Key applications include natural language processing, computer vision, and autonomous systems. The United States and China remain the leading investors, together accounting for over 60 percent of global AI funding. However, this growth faces significant challenges: the energy consumption of large language models is substantial, with some models requiring millions of GPU hours for training. Researchers at major institutions are increasingly focusing on efficiency improvements to reduce the carbon footprint of AI systems. The regulatory landscape is also evolving, with the European Union's AI Act establishing one of the first comprehensive legal frameworks for AI governance. Industry experts predict that by 2030, AI will contribute approximately 15 percent of global GDP growth, though this depends on successful implementation of safety measures and ethical guidelines across all sectors.
